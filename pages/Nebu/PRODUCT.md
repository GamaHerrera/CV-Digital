# PRODUCT.md - Nebú Social Club

## 1. Product Vision
Nebú Social Club is an underground techno and electronic music destination in Mexico. Its mantra, "We Reject The Real", sets the tone for an immersive, distorted reality where music, art, and culture collide. 

## 2. Target Audience
- Enthusiasts of techno, EBM, dark ambient, and industrial music.
- Underground culture seekers looking for authentic, raw, and non-commercial nightlife experiences.
- Patrons who appreciate exclusivity and enigmatic atmospheres.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Mysterious & Enigmatic:** The brand communicates with an air of secrecy. It doesn't over-explain; it invites discovery.
- **Raw & Direct:** Statements are bold and unapologetic (e.g., "Presencia obligatoria", "Dos noches. Una sola realidad rota.").
- **Exclusive but Anti-elite:** Focused on the music and the experience rather than superficial luxury.
