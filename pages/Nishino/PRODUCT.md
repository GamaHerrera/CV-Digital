## Users

Local diners and foodies in Zapopan/Guadalajara seeking a premium, contemporary Japanese dining experience.

## Product Purpose

Nishino Japan Dining provides an authentic, high-end Japanese culinary experience featuring Nigiris, Sashimis, and Omakase in an upscale setting.

## Positioning

Authentic Japanese Omakase and high-quality Nigiris/Sashimis in an upscale, contemporary setting in Lírica Towers, standing out through its premium approach.

## Operating Context

Reservations are handled directly via WhatsApp. Customers dine in person at Lírica Towers, Zapopan. The environment is a contemporary, upscale restaurant.
